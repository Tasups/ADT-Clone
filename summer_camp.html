Summer Camps

Every summer we hold two summer theme camps for ages 3-10. These camps incorporate a themed week with craft, snack, short movies, dancing to the theme music and an end of week performance. In the past our themes have included Princess Camp, Encanto Camp, Under the Sea Camp, Trolls, and Fairy Camp to name a few.

$150 for the week

____________

Bluey Camp | June 15-17 | 9:30am - 12pm

Bluey and Bingo welcome you to Bluey summer camp! Daily activities will begin with a morning stretch, Bluey themed arts and crafts, snack and show motivation and dances in jazz and hip hop to their favorite Bluey music. On Thursday we will have a special presentation of our dance routines to show our friends and families. Cost includes a daily snack and Bluey themed craft.

____________

K-Pop Camp | June 22-25 | 9:30am - 12pm

K-Pop Demon Hunters welcome you to K-Pop summer camp! Each day begins with a fun warm-up to get our young heroes ready for action, followed by themed arts and crafts where campers design their own demon-hunting gear and K-pop idol accessories. Campers will enjoy a snack break, then jump into high-energy dance sessions inspired by K-pop choreography and heroic adventures. Throughout the week, kids will build confidence, teamwork, and creativity as they train like true K-Pop Demon Hunters. On Thursday, we’ll host a special showcase where campers perform their routines for friends and family. Cost includes a daily snack and themed craft.

____________

Wicked Camp | August 3-6 | 9:30am - 12pm

Welcome to Wicked summer camp, where magic and friendship take center stage! Each day begins with a playful warm-up to get our young performers moving, followed by themed arts and crafts inspired by the world of Oz—think sparkling wands, emerald creations, and magical accessories. Campers will enjoy a snack break, then take part in energetic dance sessions featuring jazz and musical theater styles inspired by songs from Wicked. Throughout the week, children will build confidence, creativity, and teamwork as they explore storytelling through movement and imagination. On Thursday, we’ll host a special performance where campers showcase their routines for friends and family. Cost includes a daily snack and themed craft.
